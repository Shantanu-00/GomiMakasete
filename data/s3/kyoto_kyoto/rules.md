# Kyoto City Waste Separation & Disposal Guidelines (京都市 ごみの分け方・出し方)

- Municipality Name (EN): City of Kyoto
- Municipality Name (JA): 京都府京都市 (Kyoto-fu Kyoto-shi)
- Municipality ID: kyoto_kyoto
- Prefecture: Kyoto Prefecture (京都府)
- Governing Authority: Kyoto City Environmental Policy Bureau (京都市環境政策局)
- Enforcement Division: City Beautification Offices (まち美化事務所)
- Waste System: Mandatory Designated Paid Garbage Bag System (有料指定袋制度)
- Applicable Years: Reiwa 7 / Reiwa 8 (2025–2026 Fiscal Cycle)

---

## 1. Mandatory Designated Paid Bags & Enforcement Policy

Kyoto City enforces a statutory volume-based waste fee system. General household waste and recyclables **cannot** be disposed of in standard commercial grocery bags or generic translucent bags.

### The 2 Official Designated Paid Bags (有料指定袋)
Sold at supermarkets, convenience stores (7-Eleven, Lawson, FamilyMart), and drugstores across Kyoto City:

| Bag Type | Visual Appearance | Accepted Stream | Pricing Schedule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Combustible Bag (燃やすごみ用)** | Translucent **Yellow** with **Red** Japanese lettering | Combustible Waste (kitchen scraps, soiled paper, non-recyclables) | **5L:** ¥5 / bag<br>**10L:** ¥10 / bag<br>**20L:** ¥20 / bag<br>**30L:** ¥30 / bag<br>**45L:** ¥45 / bag<br>*(Sold in 10-bag packs)* |
| **Recyclable Resources Bag (資源ごみ用)** | Completely **Clear Transparent** with **Green** Japanese lettering | 1. Plastic Resources<br>2. Cans, Glass Bottles & PET Bottles | Same pricing as combustible bags<br>*(Sold in 5-bag packs)* |

### Strict Enforcement & Violation Rejection Stickers
- **Rejection Notice (不適正排出シール):** Trash placed in unauthorized bags, disposed of on the wrong day, or containing unsorted hazardous items will be left behind by sanitation workers. A bright warning sticker will be affixed specifying the violation reason and the collector's route unit.
- **Resident Obligation:** The resident must retrieve the rejected bag, re-sort the contents into the correct designated bag, and hold it until the next scheduled collection date.
- **Weight & Fastening Rule:** Bags must be filled only to the weight an individual can carry comfortably in one hand (maximum 10 kg) and must be tied securely at the handles. Bags taped shut or bursting at the seams are rejected.

### Timing & Curbside Net Placement
- **Morning Deadline:** Place bags out at the designated neighborhood station between sunrise and **8:00 AM** on the collection morning.
- **Night-Before Disposal:** Strictly forbidden by municipal ordinance to prevent arson and bird scavenging.
- **Crow Netting:** Bags must be placed completely underneath the neighborhood's yellow bird-proof net (*karasu netto*).

## 2. Master Waste Streams & Separation Standards

### Stream 1: Combustible Waste (燃やすごみ - Moyasu Gomi)
Collected **twice a week**. **Must use the official YELLOW Kyoto City bag.**

#### Accepted Items
- **Kitchen & Food Scraps (生ごみ):** Leftovers, raw food waste, eggshells, tea leaves, bones.
  - *Mandatory Draining:* Kyoto City operates a strict "Squeeze One More Time" (*Mō Hitoshibori*) policy. You must thoroughly squeeze and drain all excess water from kitchen scraps before placing them in the yellow bag.
- **Non-Recyclable Paper:** Soiled food paper, tissue paper, greasy cardboard/pizza boxes, carbon paper, paper cups, thermal receipts.
- **Plastics Without Recycling Value:** Dirty plastic packaging that cannot be washed clean, heavily oiled takeout trays, disposable lighters (fuel must be exhausted outdoors first).
- **Leather, Rubber & Footwear:** Shoes, sandals, boots, bags, rubber hoses.
- **Wood & Timber:** Fallen branches (cut under 50 cm and bundled), disposable wooden chopsticks (*waribashi*).
- **Glassware & Ceramics (Unique Kyoto Rule):** Broken drinking glasses, tableware, ceramic plates, incandescent light bulbs, makeup bottles.
  - *Safety Protocol:* Wrap broken shards securely in thick paper or cardboard, place them near the center of the yellow bag, and clearly write **"キケン" (KIKEN / Danger)** on the outside.

---

### Stream 2: Plastic Resources (プラスチック類)
Collected **once a week**. **Must use the official CLEAR Kyoto City bag with GREEN text.**

Covers all clean plastic containers, packaging bearing the **Pla-mark (プラマーク)**, and unified 100% plastic household goods.

#### Accepted Items
- **Containers & Packaging (プラマーク):** Plastic food trays, snack wrappers, noodle cups, condiment pump bottles, blister packs, plastic caps, label films.
- **100% Plastic Goods:** Plastic storage containers (Tupperware), buckets, basins, plastic hangers, toys, CD/DVD cases.

#### Preparation & Cleanliness Threshold
1. **The Wipe/Rinse Rule:** Lightly rinse food trays with leftover dishwater or wipe with scrap paper.
2. **Contamination Split:** Items must be empty and clean. If oil, curry, or sticky residue cannot be easily removed with a quick rinse, dispose of the item in the **Yellow Combustible Bag**. Never place soiled plastics in the green-text bag.

---

### Stream 3: Cans, Glass Bottles & PET Bottles (缶・びん・ペットボトル)
Collected **once a week**. **Must use the official CLEAR Kyoto City bag with GREEN text**, or be placed directly into neighborhood resource collection cages.

#### 1. Cans (飲料・食品用缶)
- Aluminum and steel beverage cans, canned food tins.
- **Protocol:** Empty completely and rinse with water. (18-liter square metal cans/*ittokan* must go to Oversized Waste).

#### 2. Glass Food & Beverage Bottles (飲料・食品用びん)
- Drink bottles, jam jars, condiment and liquor bottles.
- **Protocol:** Rinse inside clean. Remove caps:
  - Plastic caps $\rightarrow$ Put into **Plastic Resources** bag.
  - Metal caps $\rightarrow$ Put into **Yellow Combustible** bag.
  - *Note:* Cosmetic glass bottles and drinking cups are not food bottles; dispose of them in Combustible waste.

#### 3. PET Beverage Bottles (ペットボトル - PET 1 Mark)
- Clear plastic bottles for tea, water, soft drinks, liquor, mirin, and soy sauce.
- **3-Step Prep:**
  1. Remove cap and tear off outer label film (place cap and label into **Plastic Resources**).
  2. Rinse inside with clean water.
  3. Crush the bottle flat to conserve collection space.

---

### Stream 4: Small Metal Objects & Spray Cans (小型金属類・スプレー缶)
Collected **once a month** at neighborhood pickup points. 

#### Packaging Exception: Free Transparent Bags
- **NO Designated Paid Bag Needed:** For Small Metals, you do **not** use the yellow or green Kyoto bags. 
- Use any commercially available colorless transparent plastic bag and write **"金属" (Metal)** in bold letters on the outside.

#### Dimensional Threshold
- **Size Limit:** The longest dimension (excluding handles for pots/pans) must be **30 cm or less** (最長部分がおおむね30cm以下). Items exceeding 30 cm become **Oversized Waste**.

#### Accepted Items
- Cookware: Metal frying pans (*furaipan*), kettles, saucepans, baking sheets (all $\le$ 30 cm).
- Tools & Hardware: Scissors, kitchen knives (wrap blades safely in paper labeled "キケン"), metal cutlery, wire coat hangers, clean empty paint cans.

#### Spray Cans & Cassette Gas Cylinders (Fire Safety Protocol)
- **DO NOT PUNCTURE HOLES (穴あけ禁止):** Piercing gas cans causes collection vehicle explosions.
- **Exhaust Outdoors:** Take canisters outside into an open space away from ignition sources. Depress the nozzle until all hissing stops completely.
- Remove plastic caps (to Plastic Resources) and place empty metal canisters inside the transparent bag with small metals.

---

### Stream 5: Miscellaneous Paper Waste (雑がみ - Zatsugami) & Recyclable Paper
Collected **twice a month** by municipal crews or through local community collection groups (*Chōnaikai*).

#### Accepted Paper Categories
- **Newspapers & Flyers (新聞・チラシ):** Stack neatly and tie tightly with string.
- **Cardboard (段ボール):** Remove all plastic packing tape and shipping labels. Flatten and tie with string. (Oily pizza cartons go to Combustible).
- **Paper Beverage Cartons (紙パック):** White paper cartons with no aluminum lining. Wash, cut flat, dry, and bundle with string.
- **Miscellaneous Clean Paper (雑がみ):** Envelopes, cereal boxes, paper bags, toilet paper cores, wrapping paper, brochures.
  - *Disposal Method:* Pack inside a clean paper bag and staple/tie closed, or bundle with paper string. Alternatively, put in a transparent bag.

---

### Stream 6: Oversized Bulky Waste (大型ごみ - Oogata Gomi)

Household furniture, bedding, and large appliances exceeding standard curbside bag dimensions or the 30 cm metal limit.

#### Sizing Breakpoint
- Any metal object over **30 cm**, or any furniture/plastic/bedding item over **40 cm** that cannot be tied inside a single 45L designated bag.

#### Step-by-Step Booking & Sticker Payment
1. **Advance Reservation:**
   - Toll-Free Telephone: Kyoto Large Waste Reception Center (`0120-100-530` or `075-330-6100` via mobile).
   - Online Portal: Kyoto City Bulky Waste Web Portal (`sodai-kyoto.jp`).
   - You will receive a collection date, designated pickup spot, and total fee quotation.
2. **Purchase Waste Fee Stickers (粗大ごみ処理手数料券):**
   - Available at convenience stores across Kyoto.
   - **Denomination:** Issued in flat **¥400 units** (400円券).
   - *Example Fee Tiers:*
     - Small furniture, desk lamps, folding chairs: **¥400** (1 sticker)
     - Office chairs, microwaves, vacuum cleaners: **¥800** (2 stickers)
     - Single futon mattress, bicycles, book shelves: **¥1,200** (3 stickers)
     - Spring mattresses, large wardrobes, sofas: **¥1,600 – ¥2,400** (4 to 6 stickers)
3. **Placement:** Write your receipt number and collection date on the stickers, affix them visibly to the items, and place them outside by **8:00 AM** on your appointment date.

## 3. Nationally Banned Curbside Items (Cannot Be Collected by Kyoto City)

- **Specified Home Appliances (家電4品目):** Air Conditioners, TVs, Refrigerators/Freezers, Washing Machines/Dryers. (Recycle via retail replacement trade-in or Japan Post appliance slip).
- **Personal Computers & Laptops:** Recycle via certified postal partner Renet Japan (`renet.jp`) or the computer maker.
- **Rechargeable Batteries (Lithium-ion / Power Banks):** STRICTLY BANNED from trash bags due to extreme fire danger. Drop off at yellow JBRC battery collection bins in electronics stores or ward office eco-stations (*Eco-Machi Stations*).
- **Industrial & Hazardous Waste:** Automotive tires, car batteries, motorcycles, propane tanks, pianos, concrete/soil, medical needles.
