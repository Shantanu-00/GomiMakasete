# Shinjuku City Waste Separation & Disposal Guidelines (東京都新宿区 ごみの分け方・出し方)

- Municipality Name (EN): Shinjuku City / Shinjuku Ward
- Municipality Name (JA): 東京都新宿区 (Tokyo-to Shinjuku-ku)
- Municipality ID: tokyo_shinjuku
- Prefecture: Tokyo Metropolis (東京都)
- Governing Authority: Shinjuku City Environment and Cleansing Division (新宿区清掃事務所)
- Applicable Years: Reiwa 7 / Reiwa 8 (2025–2026 Fiscal Cycle)

---

## 1. Universal Waste Rules & Packaging Requirements

### Mandatory Bag Standards
- **Bag Type:** Colorless, transparent or semi-transparent plastic bags (45 liters or smaller).
- **No Designated City Bags:** Shinjuku City does NOT require pre-printed municipal paid bags. Any commercially sold clear or white-translucent polyethylene bag from supermarkets, 100-yen stores, or convenience stores is acceptable.
- **Prohibited Bags:** Black bags, blue opaque bags, cardboard boxes, and paper bags are strictly forbidden for curbside disposal. Crews will leave opaque bags behind with a violation notice.

### Timing & Placement Rules
- **Disposal Time:** Place waste out at the designated neighborhood collection site between sunrise and **8:00 AM** on the morning of the collection day.
- **Night-Before Prohibition:** Putting garbage out the night before is prohibited by local ward ordinance to prevent fire hazards, illegal dumping, and scavenging by crows (*karasu*) and rodents.
- **Crow Nets:** Garbage bags must be placed completely underneath the yellow crow-proof netting (*karasu netto*) provided at each station. Weigh down the net edges with rocks or water bottles.

## 2. Primary Waste Streams & Item Classification

### Stream A: Combustible Waste (燃やすごみ / 可燃ごみ - Moeru Gomi)
Collected **twice a week**. Incinerated at high temperatures in Tokyo 23 Wards Clean Authority facilities.

#### Accepted Items
- **Kitchen & Food Waste (生ごみ):** Raw food scraps, fruit peels, vegetable ends, meat bones, tea leaves, coffee grounds, eggshells. (Must be thoroughly drained of liquid).
- **Soiled Paper & Tissue:** Used facial tissues, paper towels, greasy food wrapping, thermal receipt paper, paper cups, paper plates, dirty parchment baking paper.
- **Textiles & Clothing:** Worn-out shirts, socks, underwear, trousers, ripped towels, rags.
- **Rubber & Leather Goods:** Leather shoes, belts, purses, rubber boots, rubber bands, sports balls.
- **Wooden Goods & Pruning:** Wooden chopsticks (*waribashi*), disposable wooden cutlery, toothpicks. Fallen tree leaves and garden prunings (cut branches into lengths under 50 cm, bundle with twine, maximum 3 bundles per collection).
- **Sanitary Goods:** Disposable diapers (*kamioshime* - solid fecal matter must be flushed down the toilet before disposal), sanitary napkins.
- **Contaminated Plastics:** Food packaging containers (bento boxes, curry pouches, oily instant noodle bowls, natto containers) that cannot be rinsed clean with water.

#### Preparation Protocol
1. Squeeze and drain all water out of kitchen waste before placing it into the bag. Excess water reduces incinerator efficiency and creates foul odor.
2. Leftover cooking oil must NOT be poured down drains. Soak it into scrap paper or paper towels, or solidify it using a commercially available oil-hardening powder (*Katameru Tenpu*), then place it in combustible waste.

### Stream B: Recyclable Resources (資源 - Shigen Gomi)
Collected **once a week**. Processed into recycled raw materials.

#### 1. Plastic Packaging and Containers (容器包装プラスチック)
Identified by the triangular **Pla-mark (プラマーク)** printed on the packaging.
- **Accepted Items:** Clean plastic food trays, clear strawberry/egg containers, snack and chip bags, candy wrappers, shampoo and detergent pump bottles, plastic caps, film labels, condiment squeeze tubes (mayonnaise, ketchup).
- **Preparation Protocol:**
  - Containers must be emptied and lightly rinsed with cold water or wiped with used dish tissue.
  - Squeeze tubes: Cut open and rinse clean if viscous residue remains.
  - Contamination rule: If stubborn grease or sauces cannot be easily removed with a quick rinse, dispose of the item as **Combustible Waste**.

#### 2. Used Paper (古紙 - Koshi)
Paper must be sorted into four distinct sub-streams, flattened, and securely bundled using paper string (*kamihimo*). **Do NOT use plastic packing tape or vinyl string.**
- **Cardboard (段ボール):** Corrugated shipping boxes. Remove all packing tape, delivery stickers, and shipping labels. Flatten and bundle with string. (Greasy pizza boxes are Combustible).
- **Newspapers & Flyers (新聞・チラシ):** Bundle newspapers together with advertising flyers.
- **Magazines & Books (雑誌・書籍):** Magazines, paperback books, catalogs, office copy paper.
- **Miscellaneous Clean Paper (雑がみ - Zatsugami):** Envelopes (remove plastic address windows), tissue boxes (strip plastic film around opening), clean paper bags, cereal boxes, toilet paper cardboard cores. Place inside a paper bag and staple/tie shut, or bundle with paper string.
- **Beverage Cartons (紙パック):** Milk and juice cartons with a white paper interior. Wash inside, cut open flat with scissors, dry completely, and bundle with string. (Cartons with aluminum foil lining inside are Combustible).

#### 3. Beverage & Food Cans (缶 - Kan)
- **Accepted Items:** Aluminum and steel cans for beer, soda, coffee, canned foods, pet food.
- **Preparation Protocol:** Empty contents completely, rinse inside with water, drain, and place loosely into a transparent bag or into the designated blue station basket. Do not crush cans completely flat.

#### 4. Glass Drink & Food Bottles (びん - Bin)
- **Accepted Items:** Transparent, brown, green, and blue glass bottles used for food, beverages, condiments, and liquor.
- **Preparation Protocol:**
  - Remove all metal or plastic caps and lids. (Metal caps go to Non-combustible; plastic caps go to Pla-mark).
  - Rinse inside thoroughly with water.
  - Put into transparent bags or into the yellow/orange station collection bins.
  - **Exclusions:** Cosmetic bottles, heat-resistant glassware (Pyrex), broken glass, and drinking glasses must go into **Metal, Ceramics & Glass Waste**.

#### 5. PET Beverage Bottles (ペットボトル - Petto Botoru)
Identified by the triangular **PET 1** recycling identification mark.
- **Accepted Items:** Clear plastic bottles for soft drinks, tea, water, soy sauce, cooking sake (*mirin*).
- **Mandatory 3-Step Preparation:**
  1. **Peel the outer film label** and **unscrew the plastic cap**. (Place cap and label into Pla-mark or Combustible).
  2. **Rinse the inside** with clean water.
  3. **Step on or crush the bottle flat** by hand to reduce volume, then place it in a clear bag or net container.
- **Exclusions:** Plastic bottles used for cooking oil, motor oil, or chemical detergents are NOT PET bottles; dispose of them in Combustible waste.

### Stream C: Metal, Ceramics & Glass (金属・陶器・ガラスごみ - Funen Gomi)
Collected **twice a month** (typically 1st/3rd or 2nd/4th designated day).

#### Dimensional Breakpoint
- **Strict Size Limit:** The longest edge must be **UNDER 30 cm** (30 cm未満).
- Any metal, glass, or ceramic object measuring 30 cm or greater across its longest dimension is legally classified as **Oversized Waste (粗大ごみ)** and will be rejected.

#### Accepted Items
- Small kitchen cookware: Frying pans (*furaipan*), small pots, kettles, baking sheets (all under 30 cm).
- Cutlery & utensils: Kitchen knives (*hocho*), scissors, forks, spoons, peelers.
- Tableware: Ceramic plates, bowls, coffee mugs, glassware.
- Small household hardware: Wire coat hangers, empty metal paint cans, tin foil, incandescent light bulbs.
- Mirrors and small window panes (under 30 cm).

#### Safety Packaging Mandate
- **Broken Glass, Ceramics, and Sharp Blades:** Wrap the sharp edges or shards securely in several layers of newspaper or cardboard. Write **"キケン" (KIKEN / Danger)** in large red/black letters on the outside of the transparent disposal bag to protect collection workers.

### Stream D: Hazardous, Flammable & Specially Handled Items

#### Spray Cans & Portable Gas Cartridges (スプレー缶・カセットボンベ)
- **Extreme Fire Hazard:** In Shinjuku, **DO NOT PUNCTURE HOLES** in gas canisters. Puncturing gas cans causes catastrophic fires in garbage trucks and processing plants.
- **Disposal Protocol:**
  1. Take the can outdoors to a well-ventilated area away from open flames.
  2. Depress the nozzle until the hissing sound stops completely and the canister is fully empty.
  3. Place in a **separate transparent bag by itself**. Do not mix with food cans or other metals.
  4. Write **"スプレー缶" (Spray Can)** on the bag and place out on Metal, Ceramics & Glass collection day.

#### Disposable Lighters (使い捨てライター)
- Use up all lighter fluid outdoors. Once the spark wheel no longer produces flame and no gas remains, place into Metal, Ceramics & Glass waste.

#### Dry Cell Batteries (乾電池 - Kandenchi)
- **Alkaline & Manganese Batteries (AA, AAA, C, D):** Cover both positive (+) and negative (-) terminal ends with cellophane tape to prevent short circuits. Place inside a transparent bag and put out on Metal, Ceramics & Glass collection day.
- **Lithium-ion Batteries & Power Banks (モバイルバッテリー):** STRICTLY PROHIBITED in curbside trash. They explode under truck hydraulic pressure. Take power banks, e-cigarettes/vapes, and rechargeable batteries to designated yellow JBRC recycling drop-boxes located inside electronics stores (Yodobashi Camera Shinjuku, Bic Camera) or ward municipal offices.

#### Fluorescent Light Tubes (蛍光管)
- Contains mercury. Do not break. Place in original cardboard packaging or wrap carefully and bring to municipal drop-off boxes at Shinjuku Clean Centers or local public facilities.

## 3. Oversized Bulky Waste (粗大ごみ - Sodai Gomi)

### Definition
Any household object, appliance, or furniture where the longest dimension measures **30 cm or greater** (30 cm以上).

### Mandatory Application & Collection Procedure
1. **Advance Reservation:**
   - Online Portal: Tokyo 23 Wards Sodai Gomi Reception Center (`sodai.tokyokankyo.or.jp`)
   - Telephone Hotline: Shinjuku Sodai Gomi Reception Center
   - You will receive an appointment date, collection point location, and fee quotation.
2. **Purchase Waste Processing Fee Tickets (有料粗大ごみ処理券):**
   - Available at convenience stores (7-Eleven, FamilyMart, Lawson) and supermarkets located within Shinjuku Ward.
   - **Ticket Types:**
     - **Ticket A (A券):** ¥200 per sheet
     - **Ticket B (B券):** ¥300 per sheet
   - **Important:** Stickers MUST bear the imprint **"新宿区" (Shinjuku-ku)**. Stickers purchased in neighboring wards (Shibuya, Nakano, Bunkyo) are invalid in Shinjuku and will be rejected.
3. **Affix Stickers & Put Out:**
   - Write your appointment number or full name and collection date on the stickers.
   - Attach the exact required fee combination securely onto the item.
   - Place the item outside at the front entrance of your house or apartment building's designated trash area by **8:00 AM** on the reservation day.

### Common Shinjuku Bulky Waste Fees (Reference)
- Microwave Oven: ¥400 (Ticket A x 2)
- Single Futon / Mattress (Non-spring): ¥400 (Ticket A x 2)
- Spring Mattress: ¥1,200–¥2,200 (Requires special dismantle processing)
- Office / Desk Chair: ¥400–¥800
- Bicycle (16-inch or larger): ¥800 (Ticket A x 1 + Ticket B x 2)
- Dining Table (under 180 cm): ¥1,200 (Ticket B x 4)

## 4. Legally Banned Curbside Items (Cannot Be Collected by Ward)

### The 4 Major Specified Home Appliances (家電4品目)
Under the national *Home Appliance Recycling Act*, Shinjuku City cannot collect:
1. Air Conditioners (エアコン)
2. Televisions (CRT, LCD, OLED, Plasma) (テレビ)
3. Refrigerators & Freezers (冷蔵庫・冷凍庫)
4. Washing Machines & Clothes Dryers (洗濯機・衣類乾燥機)

**How to Dispose:**
- Request trade-in removal when purchasing a replacement unit at an appliance retailer.
- Purchase a *Home Appliance Recycling Ticket (家電リサイクル券)* at a Japan Post Bank office, then arrange pickup through the Home Appliance Recycling Reception Center or drop off at a designated Tokyo transit depot.

### Personal Computers & Laptops
Under the *PC Recycling Act*, desktop towers, laptops, and LCD monitors cannot be collected curbside. Arrange free postal collection through certified e-waste partner Renet Japan (`renet.jp`) or the original computer manufacturer.

### Hazardous & Industrial Materials
Car/motorcycle tires, automotive batteries, pianos, concrete blocks, soil/sand, fire extinguishers, paint cans with chemical residue, propane cylinders. Contact specialized private disposal contractors or vendor trade-ins.
