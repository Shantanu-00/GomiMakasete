# Yokohama City Waste Separation & Disposal Guidelines (神奈川県横浜市 ごみと資源物の分け方・出し方)

- Municipality Name (EN): City of Yokohama
- Municipality Name (JA): 神奈川県横浜市 (Kanagawa-ken Yokohama-shi)
- Municipality ID: kanagawa_yokohama
- Prefecture: Kanagawa Prefecture (神奈川県)
- Governing Authority: Yokohama City Resources and Waste Recycling Bureau (横浜市資源循環局)
- Policy Framework: Yokohama 3R Dream Plan (10-Category / 15-Stream System)
- Applicable Years: Reiwa 7 / Reiwa 8 (2025–2026 Fiscal Cycle)

---

## 1. General Municipal Disposal & Bag Rules

### Bag Standards
- **Bag Type:** Transparent or semi-transparent plastic bags (45 liters or smaller).
- **No Designated Municipal Bag Required:** Yokohama City does not require pre-printed paid government bags. Commercially available translucent polyethylene bags are fully acceptable.
- **Prohibited Disposal:** Black, blue, or fully opaque bags are strictly prohibited. Waste inside opaque bags will not be collected and will receive a municipal warning sticker.
- **Sorting Compliance & Penalties:** Improperly sorted trash is left behind with a warning sticker. Under municipal ordinance, repeat offenders who ignore sorting directives face a fine of **¥2,000**. Illegal dumping carries fines up to **¥200,000**.

### Timing & Neighborhood Placement
- **Morning Deadline:** Place all waste and recyclables out at your neighborhood collection station by **8:00 AM** on the designated morning.
- **Night-Before Disposal:** Strictly prohibited. Waste left overnight attracts crows (*karasu*), feral cats, and risks arson.
- **Crow Net Protocol:** Completely cover bags with the yellow net provided at the curbside station, tucking the lead-weighted edges securely under the bags.

## 2. Waste Classification & 10 Separation Streams

### Stream 1: Burnable Garbage (燃やすごみ - Moyasu Gomi)
Collected **twice a week**.

#### Accepted Items
- **Kitchen & Food Scraps (生ごみ):** Leftovers, vegetable trimmings, fruit peels, fish bones, eggshells. Drain liquids thoroughly before disposal.
- **Contaminated Paper:** Dirty paper, paper towels, used tissue, paper cups, thermal receipt paper, photographic paper.
- **Small Non-Metal Appliances (<50 cm):** Hair dryers, curling irons, electric shavers, toasters where the longest side is under 50 cm. (Remove all dry cell/rechargeable batteries first).
- **Soiled Plastics:** Plastic packaging heavily stained with cooking oils, instant ramen grease, or sauces that cannot be rinsed clean.
- **Wood, Leather & Rubber:** Fallen twigs/branches (cut under 50 cm and bundled), leather shoes, belts, rubber boots, sneakers.
- **Sanitary Items:** Disposable diapers (solid waste must be flushed into the toilet first), sanitary products.

#### Disposal Protocol
- Place loosely into a transparent or semi-transparent bag and tie tightly.
- Solidify cooking oil using a hardening powder (*Katameru Tenpu*) or absorb into scrap paper before bagging.

---

### Stream 2: Plastic Resources (プラスチック資源 - Purasuchikku Shigen)
Collected **once a week**. Covers clean packaging with the **Pla-mark (プラマーク)** AND 100% plastic daily household products under 50 cm.

#### Accepted Items
- **Plastic Packaging with Pla-mark:** Snack bags, instant noodle wrappers, clear produce clamshells, white/colored food trays, shampoo pump bottles, detergent refill pouches, plastic caps, film labels.
- **100% Plastic Goods (Under 50 cm):** Plastic washbasins, buckets, plastic hangers, colanders, storage baskets, plastic toys, CD/DVD cases.

#### Disposal Protocol & Exceptions
- **Rinse Protocol:** Lightly rinse with leftover dishwater or wipe clean with scrap paper. If food stains cannot be washed off easily, dispose of it as **Burnable Garbage**.
- **Exclusions:** Plastic items 50 cm or larger (Oversized Garbage); hard cutting boards; plastic products combined with metal screws or wiring.

---

### Stream 3: Cans, Glass Bottles & PET Bottles (缶・びん・ペットボトル)
Collected **once a week**.

#### 1. Beverage & Food Cans (缶)
- Aluminum and steel cans that contained food, soda, beer, coffee, or pet food.
- **Protocol:** Empty completely, rinse with clean water. Do not crush completely flat.

#### 2. Glass Food & Drink Bottles (びん)
- Glass jars and bottles for alcoholic beverages, soft drinks, condiments, seasonings.
- **Protocol:** Remove metal/plastic caps. Rinse inside clean.
- **Exclusions:** Cosmetic bottles, heat-resistant glassware (Pyrex), and broken glass belong to **Non-burnable Garbage**.

#### 3. PET Bottles (ペットボトル - PET 1 Mark)
- Clear plastic bottles used for soft drinks, mineral water, green tea, cooking sake, mirin, oil-free soy sauce.
- **3-Step Prep:**
  1. Peel off outer film label and remove cap $\rightarrow$ place cap/label in **Plastic Resources**.
  2. Rinse inside with water.
  3. Crush the bottle flat to conserve collection space.

---

### Stream 4: Small Metal Items (小さな金属類 - Chiisana Kinzokurui)
Collected **twice a month**.

#### Strict Dimensional Cutoff
- **Size Limit:** The longest dimension must be **UNDER 30 cm** (30 cm未満).
- Cookware (pots, frying pans, kettles) is measured based on the **diameter of the rim, excluding the handle**. If the rim is under 30 cm, it is Small Metal.

#### Accepted Items
- Metal frying pans, saucepans, kettles, aluminum foil, metal hangers, cutlery, scissors, umbrella frames (if under 30 cm when collapsed), clean empty paint cans.

#### Disposal Protocol
- With the exception of very small items like spoons and screws, place items out **as is without a bag**, or in a transparent bag directly at the station.

---

### Stream 5: Non-Burnable Garbage (燃えないごみ - Moenai Gomi)
Collected **twice a month**.

#### Accepted Items
- Ceramics and pottery (plates, mugs, teacups, clay pots).
- Glassware (drinking glasses, heat-resistant Pyrex, cosmetic and medicine glass bottles).
- Incandescent light bulbs (wrap in original cardboard packaging or newspaper).
- Broken window glass and mirrors.

#### Safety Packaging Mandate
- Shards and sharp broken glass must be wrapped safely in newspaper or placed inside their original cardboard box. 
- You must write **"キケン" (KIKEN / Danger)** clearly on the outside of the bag.

---

### Stream 6: Spray Cans & Cassette Gas Cylinders (スプレー缶・カセットボンベ)
Collected on the same day as Cans/Bottles, but handled under strict fire safety protocols.

#### Mandatory Fire Prevention Protocol
- **DO NOT PUNCTURE HOLES:** Never pierce or punch holes into gas canisters. It causes fires and explosions inside garbage collection vehicles.
- **Exhaust Contents:** Take the canister outside into an open, well-ventilated area away from any flame. Press the nozzle until the hissing sound stops completely and no pressure remains.
- **Separate Packaging:** Remove the plastic cap (put into Plastic Resources). Place exhausted metal gas canisters into a **separate transparent bag by themselves**. Do not mix with food cans.

---

### Stream 7: Dry Cell Batteries (乾電池等)
Collected at collection sites or designated municipal collection boxes.

#### Accepted Types
- Alkaline and manganese dry cells (AA, AAA, C, D), cylindrical lithium batteries, coin-type batteries (CR/BR series).

#### Safety Protocol
- Insulate the positive (+) and negative (-) terminals with cellophane or electrical tape to prevent friction sparks and fire.
- Place batteries in a small transparent bag.
- **Button cells (LR/SR/PR) and Rechargeable Lithium-ion batteries:** Take directly to cooperating electronics retailers (Bic Camera, Yodobashi) with yellow JBRC recycling drop-boxes.

---

### Stream 8: Used Paper (古紙 - Koshi)
Collected **once a week** (handled by local recycling contracts). Must be separated into **4 distinct piles** and tied with string. **Do not use adhesive tape.**

1. **Cardboard (段ボール):** Strip off plastic packing tape and shipping label slips. Flatten flat and tie with paper string.
2. **Newspapers & Advertisements (新聞・チラシ):** Stack together and tie with string.
3. **Magazines & Books (雑誌・書籍):** Catalogs, books, magazines bundled with string.
4. **Paper Cartons / Milk Packs (紙パック):** White paper cartons with no aluminum interior. Wash inside, cut open flat, dry, and tie with string.
5. **Other Miscellaneous Paper (その他の紙):** Clean food boxes, paper bags, envelopes, paper towel tubes. Pack inside a paper bag and tie the handles with string.

---

### Stream 9: Used Textiles & Clothing (古布 - Kohu)
Collected **once a week** by citizen recycling groups.

#### Accepted Items
- Clean, wearable shirts, trousers, jackets, curtains, towels, sheets, blankets.

#### Critical Weather Rule
- Wash and dry items thoroughly. Pack in a transparent bag.
- **RAIN CANCELLATION:** Never put textiles out on rainy days. Wet clothes turn moldy and must be incinerated instead of reused. Hold items until the next dry collection day.

---

### Stream 10: Oversized Garbage (粗大ごみ - Sodai Gomi)
Applies to large household items exceeding municipal size cutoffs.

#### Dimensional Breakpoint Matrix
| Material Type | Threshold Dimension | Action Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Metal Objects** | **Longest side is 30 cm or greater** | Must book as Oversized Garbage |
| **Non-Metal (Wood, Plastic, Mixed)** | **Longest side is 50 cm or greater** | Must book as Oversized Garbage |

#### Booking & Payment Steps
1. **Advance Reservation:**
   - Web: Yokohama City Bulky Waste Online Portal (`yokohama-sodai.jp`)
   - Chat: Yokohama City LINE Official Account
   - Telephone: Yokohama Oversized Garbage Information Center (0570-200-530 / 045-330-3953)
2. **Purchase Disposal Stickers (粗大ごみ処理手数料シール):**
   - Purchase stickers at Yokohama convenience stores, post offices, or ward offices displaying the official municipal seal.
   - Denominations: Tiered fee stickers (typically ¥200, ¥500, ¥1,000, ¥1,500 depending on item size and type).
3. **Placement:** Write your receipt number on the sticker, attach it to the item, and place it outside your home entrance by **8:00 AM** on the assigned appointment date.

## 3. Items Not Collected by Yokohama City

Under Japanese statutory laws, the following items will not be collected under curbside or bulky trash:
- **Specified Home Appliances (家電4品目):** Air Conditioners, Televisions, Refrigerators/Freezers, Washing Machines/Clothes Dryers. (Recycle via retail store trade-in or Japan Post recycling ticket).
- **Personal Computers:** Desktop units, laptops, LCD screens. (Recycle via manufacturer or postal e-waste service Renet Japan).
- **Hazardous / Industrial Items:** Motorcycle parts, car tires, car batteries, fire extinguishers, concrete blocks, gas propane tanks, medical sharps/syringes.
