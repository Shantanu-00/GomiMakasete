# Kamikatsu Town Zero Waste Guidelines (徳島県上勝町 ゼロ・ウェイスト分別ガイド)

- Municipality Name (EN): Town of Kamikatsu
- Municipality Name (JA): 徳島県上勝町 (Tokushima-ken Kamikatsu-chō)
- Municipality ID: tokushima_kamikatsu
- Prefecture: Tokushima Prefecture (徳島県)
- Facility: Kamikatsu Zero Waste Center "WHY?" / Hibigaya Garbage Station (上勝町ゼロ・ウェイストセンター)
- Policy: Zero Waste Declaration (13 Master Streams / 45 Granular Categories)
- Applicable Years: Reiwa 7 / Reiwa 8 (2025–2026 Fiscal Cycle)

---

## 1. Foundational Municipal Waste Rules

### Zero Curbside Truck Collection (ゴミ収集車なし)
- **No Door-to-Door Pickup:** Kamikatsu operates **zero curbside garbage trucks** for residential homes.
- **Station Drop-Off System:** Residents and lodging visitors must transport all household waste directly to the central **Kamikatsu Zero Waste Center (Why? Station)**.
- **Operating Hours:** Open daily from **07:30 AM to 02:00 PM** (365 days a year, with adjusted hours during New Year holidays).

### Bag & Separation Policy
- **No Municipal Trash Bags:** Trash bags are not thrown into bins. Waste is brought in reusable boxes, buckets, or bags, and emptied by hand into dedicated sorting crates and drums at the station.
- **Self-Sorting Requirement:** Sanitation staff supervise the station, but individuals must personally deposit items into the correct crates.
- **Cost Transparency Ledger:** Every crate displays a placard indicating the cost to the town (出 / Expense) or revenue generated (入 / Revenue) per kilogram, and what industrial material the waste becomes.

### The Home-Composting Mandate (Kitchen Waste)
- **Zero Raw Food at Station:** Raw kitchen food waste (生ごみ - *namagomi*) is **strictly prohibited** at the Zero Waste Center.
- **Home Composting:** 100% of households process kitchen scraps on-site using subsidized electric food dehydrators or non-electric microorganism soil boxes (*Kieero* / キエーロ).

## 2. Complete 13 Master Streams & 45 Granular Categories

### Master Stream 1: Paper Resources (紙類 - 9 Categories)
All paper must be clean and dry. Flatten and stack by category.

1. **Newspapers & Flyers (新聞・チラシ):** Newsprint and bundled promotional inserts.
2. **Corrugated Cardboard (段ボール):** Boxes with wavy corrugated fluting. Packing tape and delivery stickers must be removed.
3. **Magazines & Books (雑誌・本):** Catalogs, softcover books, weekly magazines, paperback books.
4. **Hardcover Book Covers (硬い紙芯・本の表紙):** Thick cloth/pressed cardboard covers ripped off from hardcover books, heavy cardboard mailing tubes.
5. **Paper Cartons with White Interior (紙パック・白):** Milk and juice cartons with pure white paper inside. Wash, cut open flat, dry.
6. **Paper Cartons with Aluminum Foil Interior (紙パック・銀):** Shelf-stable beverage and sake cartons lined with silver aluminum foil. Wash, cut open, and dry.
7. **Paper Cups (紙カップ・白):** Clean paper drinking cups with no food encrustation.
8. **Shredded Paper (シュレッダーくず):** Securely bagged in clear bags to prevent blowing in wind.
9. **Miscellaneous Mixed Clean Paper (その他の紙・雑がみ):** Envelopes (remove cellophane address windows), tissue boxes, cardboard food sleeves, clean paper wrapping.

---

### Master Stream 2: Metal Resources (金属類 - 5 Categories)
All containers must be rinsed and drained.

10. **Aluminum Cans (アルミ缶):** Beverage cans marked with the aluminum symbol. Rinse thoroughly.
11. **Steel Cans (スチール缶):** Food tins, tea cans, pet food tins.
12. **Spray Cans & Cassette Gas Cylinders (スプレー缶・ボンベ):** Exhaust completely outdoors until hissing stops. Puncturing is permitted at the station only using the dedicated mechanical puncture tool provided by staff.
13. **Metal Bottle Caps (金属製キャップ):** Beer crown caps, metal jam jar lids, screw-off wine caps.
14. **Mixed Scrap Metals (雑金属):** Frying pans, kettles, metal ladles, umbrella skeletons, wire hangers, bicycle rims, scrap iron/copper pipes.

---

### Master Stream 3: Plastic Resources (プラスチック類 - 6 Categories)

15. **Plastic Packaging & Containers (プラスチック製容器包装):** Clean packaging bearing the **Pla-mark (プラマーク)**. Food trays, snack bags, condiment tubes, wrapping films. Must be washed clean; if oily, wipe before rinsing.
16. **Other Plastics / Waste Plastics (その他のプラスチック・廃プラ):** Non-packaging plastics or items where stains/oils cannot be washed off (curry pouches, meat wrap, toothbrushes, plastic cutlery, cassette tapes).
17. **PET Beverage Bottles (ペットボトル):** Clear drink bottles with PET 1 mark. Wash, remove cap and outer film label, step on to crush flat.
18. **Plastic Bottle Caps (プラスチック製キャップ):** Screw caps from PET drink bottles and condiment containers.
19. **White Expanded Polystyrene (白色発泡スチロール):** Clean white meat/fish trays and pure white shipping styrofoam blocks. Break into manageable pieces.
20. **Colored / Printed Styrofoam (有色発泡スチロール):** Colored meat trays, printed noodle bowls made of foam polystyrene.

---

### Master Stream 4: Glass Bottles (びん類 - 4 Categories)
Remove all metal or plastic caps before sorting. Rinse bottles clean.

21. **Clear Transparent Glass Bottles (透明びん):** Colorless drink and condiment jars.
22. **Brown Glass Bottles (茶色びん):** Amber medicine bottles, brown beverage jars.
23. **Other Colored Glass Bottles (その他の色のびん):** Green wine bottles, blue bottles, black liquor jars.
24. **Returnable Bottles (一升びん・ビールびん):** Standard brown/green 1.8-liter sake bottles (*isshōbin*) and brewery beer bottles. Kept intact in wooden crates for commercial deposit refunds.

---

### Master Stream 5: Hazardous & Dangerous Items (危ないもの類 - 6 Categories)

25. **Ceramics & Heat-Resistant Glass (陶磁器・ガラス類):** Broken dishware, ceramic mugs, clay pots, Pyrex, drinking glasses. (Crushed for roadbed foundation material).
26. **Mirrors & Mercury Thermometers (鏡・水銀体温計):** Glass mirrors, old mercury-based medical thermometers (kept intact in hazardous containment).
27. **Incandescent Bulbs & Fluorescent Tubes (電球・蛍光灯):** Straight tubes, circular fluorescent bulbs, LED lamps. Wrap in original packaging to prevent breakage.
28. **Dry Cell Batteries (乾電池):** Alkaline and manganese batteries (AA, AAA, C, D). Terminals taped with cellophane.
29. **Rechargeable & Automotive Batteries (廃バッテリー):** Lithium-ion power banks, small lead-acid batteries, motorcycle batteries.
30. **Disposable Lighters (ライター):** Gas lighters with all combustible fluid exhausted.

---

### Master Stream 6: Textiles & Fabric (布類 - 2 Categories)

31. **Wearable Clothing & Blankets (衣類・毛布):** Clean shirts, trousers, winter coats, wool blankets. Must be clean and dry.
32. **Other Fabrics & Scrap Cloth (その他の布類):** Non-wearable rags, torn sheets, fabric scraps, curtains.

---

### Master Stream 7: Biomass & Timber (バイオマス・木類 - 2 Categories)

33. **Wood Scraps & Wooden Chopsticks (木くず・割り箸):** Disposable wooden chopsticks (*waribashi*), toothpicks, small untreated lumber trimmings under 50 cm.
34. **Used Cooking Oil (廃食油):** Leftover tempura and vegetable frying oil. Filter out food sediment and pour liquid into the station's dedicated oil barrels. (Refined into municipal biodiesel fuel).

---

### Master Stream 8: Oversized Bulky Items (粗大ごみ - 4 Categories)

35. **Metal Bulky Goods (金属製粗大ごみ):** Steel bed frames, metal desks, steel cabinets, exercise machines.
36. **Wooden Bulky Goods (木製粗大ごみ):** Solid wood tables, chairs, dressers, wooden shelving.
37. **Bedding & Floor Coverings (布団・絨毯・畳):** Futon mattresses, wool carpets, synthetic area rugs, woven tatami mats.
38. **Large Rubber & PVC Products (塩ビ製品・ゴム製品など):** Agricultural PVC pipes, vinyl sheets, large rubber hoses, inflatables.

---

### Master Stream 9: Functional Reuse ("Kuru Kuru Shop" / くるくるショップ)

39. **Reusable Household Goods (リユース品):** Tableware, cups, children's toys, baby clothes, intact small appliances, decor that still function. Residents and visitors can take these items home for **free**.

---

### Master Stream 10: Non-Recyclable Landfill & Incineration Residue (燃やさざるを得ないごみ - 6 Categories)
Items that cannot be chemically recycled, repurposed, or processed under current technology. This represents less than 20% of town waste, transported out-of-town for thermal recovery.

40. **Rubber Goods (ゴム類):** Rubber boots, rubber bands, diving suits.
41. **Leather Products (革製品):** Worn leather shoes, belts, bags beyond repair.
42. **Disposable Diapers (紙おむつ):** Fecal matter flushed in toilet; diapers bagged and placed in sanitary containment.
43. **Sanitary & Feminine Hygiene Products (生理用品):** Non-recyclable sanitary pads.
44. **Composite Multi-Layer Waste (複合素材ごみ):** Pill blister packs, insulated cooler bags, dirty composite makeup containers.
45. **Pet Waste & Heavily Contaminated Sweepings (ペット砂・灰):** Cat litter, floor sweepings, fireplace wood ash.

## 3. Station Cleanliness & Rejection Protocols

1. **The Washing Rule:** Containers with food residue (mirin, soy sauce, mayonnaise, oil) are rejected at the gate. Everything must be washed with leftover water and dried before arrival.
2. **Dismantling Rule:** Multi-material objects must be disassembled at home. For example, strip the vinyl cloth from an umbrella frame:
   - Steel skeleton $\rightarrow$ Category 14 (Mixed Scrap Metals)
   - Vinyl fabric $\rightarrow$ Category 16 (Waste Plastics)
